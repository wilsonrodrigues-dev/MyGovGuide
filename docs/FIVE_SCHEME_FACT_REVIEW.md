## IN-WK-02 — Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

Official evidence: [DFS PMJJBY FAQ: benefit, premium, entry and enrolment sections](https://financialservices.gov.in/pmjjby). Inspected 2026-11-18T08:38:58.332Z. No dependable publication date asserted.

- Benefit: One-year renewable life cover of INR 200000 for death from any cause, subject to policy terms. Standard annual renewal premium INR 436; first-time mid-year entry has pro-rata options. A 30-day non-accidental-death lien applies on first entry.
- Audience: Individual account holders of participating banks/post offices; entry age 18 to 50. Continued cover to age 55 is subject to renewal and policy conditions. No personal eligibility conclusion.
- Application: Request enrolment with a participating bank/post office using its prescribed form and consent to premium auto-debit. Confirm applicable first-entry premium and terms.
- Documents: Prescribed enrolment and auto-debit consent/declaration form
- Missing/review: Bank-specific identity/KYC checklist not verified; Full insurance policy/claims exclusions and all amendments need editorial review; Official FAQ lists last updated 2026-01-05; retrieval alone is not certification of later policy changes
- Rights basis for review: Public factual paraphrase with a short evidence quotation; no copied imagery/expression; human rights/currentness review required.

- Rights basis for review: Public factual paraphrase and short evidence quotation; human rights/currentness review required.
