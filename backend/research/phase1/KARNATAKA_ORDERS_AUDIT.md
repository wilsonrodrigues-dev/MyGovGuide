### Additional leads, not operative approvals

- [Karnataka Legislative Council draft Yuva Nidhi guidelines (2024)](https://kla.kar.nic.in/council/Replies/unstarred/19022024/usq403%2819022024%29.pdf) — labelled **Draft**; cannot be assumed to govern 2026.
- [Karnataka five-guarantees circular index on OpenCity (2023)](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/government-of-karnataka-five-guarantees-2023) — third-party archival copies of historic orders; useful only to locate dates/numbers before obtaining official current amendments.
- [Bengaluru Urban district scheme directory](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/schemes/) — useful as a government-operated source directory.

### Required sign-off evidence for each

Record official GO/circular number; department; signed date; effective date; operative amendment list; official PDF URL; issuing department; exact clause/page; reviewer; and `ruleVerificationDate`. No active rule set without this evidence. Records are not proof of eligibility for a user until approved and current.
